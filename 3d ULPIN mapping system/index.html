A NEW DIMENSION FOR LAND RECORDS & CONFLICT RESOLUTION

India’s 14-digit ULPIN (Unique Land Parcel Identification Number) concept pinpoints land parcels. This prototype extends it by providing search-by-ULPIN Google Maps navigation, floor-level 3D spatial units, and exact geometric overlap measurement in square meters and square feet to detect property boundary disputes before registration.

3D ULPIN & Overlap Analysis

This prototype is created by Team G to demonstrate how India’s ULPIN (Bhu-Aadhaar) can be visualized in an interactive 3D spatial interface:

  • Instant ULPIN Search Engine: Search any 14-digit ULPIN, building name, or floor unit with instant map pan/zoom focus.
  • Automated Boundary Overlap Measurement: Calculates exact encroached area in m² and sq ft, linear dimensions, and percentage of parcel affected.
  • Vertical & Subsurface Spatial Units: Exploded views of floor sub-ULPINs (e.g. F01, F02) and underground utility lines.
Team G Project Notice Shantivan Layout and all ULPIN codes are fictional representations designed for academic research by Team G.
